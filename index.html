Plan your row.
Know where to mark.

HangMarks calculates support points for a single row of picture frames. Mix frame sizes, choose equal gaps, and measure every mark from the same origin.

Version 1.0 is in preparation. The app is not yet available on the App Store.

Get started

  1. Enter the wall width, wall height, row height, and gap.
  2. For each frame, enter its outside dimensions and one or two support points. Measure points from the frame’s left edge as it will hang and down from its top.
  3. Choose center or top alignment. The row is centered horizontally on the wall.
  4. Open Marks. X starts at the wall’s left edge; Y starts at the floor. Export a PDF if you need a separate installation sheet.

Contact support

Why do some measurements start with ≈?

Display values are rounded to 0.1 mm or the nearest 1/16 inch. Each point is calculated from full precision values. Measure every point from the same origin instead of adding rounded gaps.

Are these drill-hole positions?

They are the points where the wall hardware supports the frame. A bracket’s fixing holes may be offset from that position. Account for your hardware before drilling. HangMarks does not detect studs or wires, assess walls, or select load-rated fixings.

Can I use picture wire?

Measure wire held taut in its loaded position. For two support points, both must be at the same height. This version plans one horizontal row; it does not arrange a multi-row gallery.

Where are my plans?

Plans are stored in the app on your device. There is no account or cloud sync. Removing the app can remove saved plans. PDF export preserves a readable sheet, but it is not an editable backup that can be imported into the app.

挂点尺使用帮助

为一排不同尺寸的画框计算挂点位置。输入墙面、画框外沿、框间净空及挂点尺寸，选择中心线或顶边对齐，即可得到横向居中的布局。

  1. 先确认画框挂好后哪一边是左侧，再测量背面的支撑点。
  2. 每个 X 从墙左边量起，每个 Y 从地面量起，避免累加舍入误差。
  3. ≈ 表示显示值经过舍入：毫米到 0.1，英寸到 1/16。
  4. 结果是支撑挂点，不一定是螺丝孔。打孔前计入五金偏移，并自行确认墙体与五金适用。

方案仅保存在设备内，无账号、无云同步。PDF 是可阅读的安装单，不能重新导入编辑。当前版本仅支持一排画框；双挂点须同高。
