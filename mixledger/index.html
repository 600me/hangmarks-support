Batch ratios, recorded clearly

MixLedger is an offline iPhone and iPad tool for planning and recording batches from a ratio you supply. One-time purchase, no account, ads or tracking. English and Simplified Chinese.

Plan a batch

  1. Enter a batch name and two to six component names and positive ratio parts, such as A 2 and B 1.
  2. Choose the ratio basis from the manufacturer's label: mass or volume. Choose whether you will measure in grams or millilitres. When those bases differ, enter a positive density in g/mL for each component; do not guess it.
  3. Choose a total amount, or select a component and enter its known amount in the chosen measurement unit.
  4. For mass readings, enter the container mass, or 0 if your scale is tared. Tap Plan this batch.

Readings and stock

“Add” is the amount for that component. “Cumulative reading” is the running total, including the entered container mass for mass measurements. For a 300 g batch at a mass ratio of 2:1 with a tared scale, add 200 g of A, then 100 g of B; the cumulative readings are 200 g and 300 g.

Optional stock uses the measurement unit. Enter stock for every component to calculate the largest possible batch and its limiting components. A shortage is the difference between target and available stock. Changing measurement basis clears stock and actual entries to avoid retaining values in the wrong unit.

Actual amounts and records

Enter actual additions to compare their normalized shares against the declared ratio in percentage points. These are numerical differences, not a curing or suitability assessment. Editing a recipe clears actual additions. Tap Save batch snapshot to keep an immutable copy of the current settings and entries on your device. Open the Saved tab to reopen or delete records. Up to 500 snapshots can be stored; export or remove old records when the limit is reached.

PDF export

Export the current batch, a saved batch or all saved records through Apple's share sheet. Reports include the ratio basis, measurement unit, target additions, cumulative readings and entered stock/density/actual values. Longer records continue across pages; each saved record starts a new page.

Boundaries

Use your material manufacturer's ratio, basis and density information. The initial sample is an example, not a brand recipe. MixLedger does not assess curing, material suitability or medical use and does not connect to Bluetooth scales. Displayed values are rounded to 0.001; calculations retain full precision. The supported computed range is 0.000001 to 1 billion in the chosen unit. Invalid or incomplete input is reported instead of silently estimated.

Storage help and support

Records are stored only on the device. Removing the app can remove records; use PDF export before changing devices. A storage error keeps existing data and blocks overwriting it. Do not delete the app as a first repair step.

Report a MixLedger issue. Reports on GitHub are public: include the app version, device and steps, but no private formulas, personal information or files you cannot share publicly.

使用方法

填写批次名称、2至6种组分名称与正数比例份数。比例依据按厂家标签选择重量或体积；测量单位选择克或毫升。两种依据不一致时，每种材料都需填写准确的正数密度（g/mL），不要猜测。

按整批总量或已知组分用量计算。重量测量填写容器重量；电子秤已去皮则填0。点击“计算批次”。添加量为本项用量，累计读数为逐项累计值并计入容器重量。例如去皮后按重量2:1配300g，依次添加200g和100g，累计读数为200g和300g。

库存以当前测量单位填写；每项均填写后可算最多可配批次和受限组分。切换测量依据会清空库存及实际用量。实际添加量用于比较实际份额与填写配方的百分点偏差，不判断固化或材料适用性。编辑配方会清空实际记录。

保存批次快照后可从“记录”重开或删除，最多500条。可导出当前批次、已存单批或全部批次的分页PDF，通过系统分享面板保存。记录仅在本机；卸载可能丢失数据，换设备前请导出。遇存储错误时保留已有数据，不要先卸载应用。

应用不连接蓝牙秤，不提供厂家配方。请按材料标签填写比例与密度，显示值保留0.001、计算保留完整精度。问题请用上方客服入口反馈；GitHub反馈公开，请勿提交私人配方或个人信息。
