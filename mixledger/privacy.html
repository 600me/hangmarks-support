Privacy policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026. Applies to MixLedger 1.0 for iPhone and iPad.

Data in the app

MixLedger does not collect or transmit personal data to its developer. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, account system, third-party SDK or developer-operated server. The app makes no network requests.

Batch and component names, ratios, density, stock and actual measurements are stored locally on your device when you save a batch snapshot. Saved records can be deleted in the app. Removing the app can remove its data. Device backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings; the developer cannot access them.

PDF export

When you choose to export, the native share sheet sends a PDF only to the destination you select. The file includes the batch names, ratios and values you entered and the calculated batch sheet. The chosen service handles the file under its own privacy practices.

Website and support

This site uses GitHub Pages without advertising, analytics scripts or application cookies. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to provide its service under its privacy statement.

GitHub Issues reports are public and contain your GitHub profile and whatever you post. The developer reads reports to provide support. Avoid private formulas and personal information; you can edit your report or request removal in the same issue.

For questions, use MixLedger support. Changes to the app's data practices will be reflected in this policy before or alongside the change.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026年10月8日，适用于 MixLedger 1.0。

应用不向开发者收集或传输个人数据，不含广告、统计、跟踪、账号系统、第三方SDK或开发者服务器，不发起网络请求。保存的批次及组分名称、比例、密度、库存和实际用量仅存于本机，可在应用中删除。卸载可能移除数据。系统备份由Apple及设备设置管理，开发者无法访问。

主动导出PDF时，系统分享面板仅向你选择的位置发送文件，文件包含批次名称、填写的配方与数值及计算批次单。接收服务按自身隐私规则处理。

网站由GitHub Pages托管，无广告、统计脚本或应用Cookie，GitHub可能处理IP等技术数据。GitHub Issues反馈公开并包含你的GitHub资料与自愿提交的内容，请勿提交私人配方或个人信息。可编辑自己的反馈或在原反馈请求移除。隐私问题可通过上方客服入口提出，数据处理变化时本政策会同步或提前更新。
