Privacy policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026
Applies to HangMarks 1.0 for iPhone and iPad.

Data handled by the app

HangMarks does not collect or transmit your personal data to its developer. It does not contain advertising, tracking, analytics, third-party SDKs, an account system, or a developer-operated server.

Frame names, measurements, layouts and saved plans remain in the app’s local storage. The language setting is saved in the app’s own preferences. The app makes no network requests.

Your choices

You can delete saved plans in the app. Removing the app can remove its local data. Device backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings; the developer cannot access them.

When you choose to export a PDF, the system share sheet sends that file only to the destination you choose. The PDF includes the plan name, frame names and measurements. Handling by the chosen service is subject to that service’s privacy practices.

This website and support

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, has no analytics or advertising code, and sets no application cookies. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to operate its service under the GitHub privacy statement.

Support requests submitted through GitHub Issues are public and include your GitHub profile and anything you choose to post. The developer reads these reports to provide support. Do not include private information. You can edit your report or request removal through the same issue.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the HangMarks support page. If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before or alongside that change.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 6 日。适用于 iPhone / iPad 上的 HangMarks（挂点尺）1.0。

应用不会向开发者收集或传输个人数据，不含广告、跟踪、统计、第三方 SDK、账号系统或开发者服务器，不发起联网请求。

画框名称、尺寸、布局与已存方案保存在本机应用内；语言偏好使用应用自身的设置存储。可在应用中删除已存方案。卸载应用可能删除本地数据。系统备份由 Apple 和你的设备设置控制，开发者无法访问。

主动导出 PDF 时，系统分享面板仅将文件发送到你选择的位置。PDF 包含方案名称、画框名称和尺寸；所选服务按其自身隐私规则处理文件。

本帮助站由 GitHub Pages 托管，无广告或统计代码，不设置应用 Cookie。GitHub 可能按其隐私声明处理 IP 地址等运行数据。GitHub Issues 反馈是公开的，会包含你的 GitHub 资料及自愿提交的内容，开发者据此处理问题；请勿提交私人信息，可编辑自己的反馈或在原反馈内请求移除。

隐私相关问题请通过 客服入口提出。数据处理方式发生变化时，本政策会在变化前或同时更新。
