RollLedger assigns full-height wallpaper drops to rolls and produces a cutting and hanging sheet. Version 1.0 is in preparation and is not yet available on the App Store.

Plan a job

  1. Enter each rectangular wall’s width and height, using millimeters or inches. Enter the dimensions printed on the roll label.
  2. Select free, straight or half-drop matching and enter the pattern repeat where applicable. Set trimming allowances for the top and bottom of each drop.
  3. Build the cut plan. W1-S1 means wall 1, strip 1. Cut from the indicated roll and hang drops in wall order.
  4. Check off cuts, save your project, or export a PDF sheet using the system share menu.

Understand the estimate

The app counts full-height drops; it does not subtract door or window areas. A patterned roll reserves one complete repeat to establish its starting reference. Positions on the list start after this reserve. Each wall restarts its matching sequence.

Earlier roll tails can be reused for shorter drops. This is a conservative assignment, not a guarantee of the smallest possible roll count. Check actual print direction, manufacturer instructions, roll lengths and batch labels before cutting. Murals, paired A/B roll sets and angled ceilings are outside this version’s scope.

Saved projects and PDF sheets

Projects and checked cuts remain on your device. Changing a project name or switching measurement units preserves checks; changing physical dimensions resets them because the cutting list can change. Removing the app can remove saved projects. A PDF is a readable sheet, not an editable backup that can be imported.

Limits: 20 walls and 200 drops per project, 100 saved projects. Use the New project action in Saved to start another job.

Support

Report a RollLedger problem

A free GitHub account is needed to submit a report. Reports are public. Include the app version and a small example of the measurements and matching mode; do not include your home address, bank information or private photos.

墙纸裁切使用帮助

  1. 输入各面矩形墙的宽高及卷标上的墙纸宽度、长度，单位可选毫米或英寸。
  2. 选择无对花、平行对花或半错位对花；按需要填写花距和每条上下裁边。
  3. 生成分卷清单。W1-S1 表示第1面墙第1条；按纸卷裁切，再按墙面编号安装。
  4. 逐条勾选已裁记录，保存项目，或通过系统分享菜单导出 PDF。

按整高裁条，不扣除门窗面积。每卷有图案墙纸先预留一个完整花距确定匹配基准，清单卷内位置从预留后的基准起算；每面墙独立开始对花顺序。可复用前卷余料，但不保证最少卷数。裁切前请核对实际图案、厂家说明及批次卷标。

不支持壁画、A/B成对卷或斜顶。每项目最多20面墙、200条，可保存100个项目。改名和切换单位保留勾选；实际尺寸改变会重置勾选。项目只在本机保存，卸载应用可能删除数据；PDF不能重新导入编辑。
